# V13 — A Life Worth Living: interface map and migration plan

## What the play screen is today (12.4)

All of this lives in `src/ShinobiLife.jsx`, inside `ShinobiLife()`.

- **Screens**: `intro` (character creation), `play`, `dead`.
- **Play screen layers**, back to front:
  1. Background: village or era art, the chakra field, grain and vignette (`ATMOS`, `bgMode`).
  2. Cinematic overlays: investiture, Mangekyo flash, Madara scenes, Infinite Tsukuyomi,
     Sharingan awakening, "moments", kill flash, village arrival, the year odometer.
  3. One of two interface skins (`ui` preference): **The Dossier** (default) or **The Scroll**.
  4. 62 modal screens (`modal === "..."`), the battle screen (`bt`), forced decisions (`c.dilemma`).
- **The Dossier header** shows all of this at once: identity and year, a power ring, a vitals rail
  (health, standing or infamy, ryo, four action pips, Age up), a world-state ticker with five
  indicators, eight hub doors (`V11_HUBS`), about nineteen quick-jump chips, a war bar, a news bar
  and an "ongoing" strip (`situationOf`).
- **The Dossier body**: the body and the record (8 stats, about 35 conditional record lines), the
  life feed, the action deck (`acts`: "Needs you now", "This year", "The world", about 20 tiles),
  a dock (People, Profile, Age up), the Times, the world at a glance and the changelog, plus a
  floating Age up button.
- **Duplication**: the War is reachable from five places (deck tile, quick chip, war bar, hub,
  ongoing strip); the Bingo Book from four; People from three. A genin sees Kage-level tools
  (Rule, Tribunal, Court) as disabled tiles competing with the three things they can actually do.

## What already works in our favour

- Every action is data (`acts`), decided in one place.
- `situationOf(c)` already ranks everything still happening, worst first, with a `go` target.
- The hub screen (`modal === "hub"`) already groups the deep systems into Life, World, People,
  Records, Power, Politics, Society and Law.
- `goTo()` opens any screen from a target description.

## V13.0 — The New Life (this release)

A new presentation layer over the existing systems. No simulation, combat, save or story code is
rewritten; every button opens the screen or runs the function it already did.

1. **New default interface, `ui === "life"`.** The Dossier and the Scroll stay available under
   Appearance → Interface. Stored preferences are migrated once (`v13` flag), so existing players
   land in the new interface, and their choice is respected afterwards.
2. **Five destinations** replace the header clutter: Home, Explore, Character, World, Chronicle.
   Top tabs on wide screens, a bottom tab bar on phones. One compact "Advance year" control is
   always reachable. A **More** sheet lists every screen in the game, grouped by destination, so
   nothing becomes undiscoverable.
3. **Home** answers: who am I, what matters, what next. The new office art, identity and status in
   words, a life chapter (The Beginning, The Shinobi, The Path, The Legacy), **one** "Your next
   chapter" event with a primary action, up to three suggestions, up to four pending matters
   (from `situationOf`), recent developments, and Advance year with a plain list of what will
   resolve. After a year turns, a **Year in review** card shows only the most important lines.
4. **Explore**: places, not menus. The training grounds, the mission desk, the market, the
   streets, the border, the tower, the clan compound, the tribunal and court, the bounty station;
   for missing-nin the underworld and the hideout. Each place offers only its own actions.
5. **Character**: the body, abilities and advanced powers (only when you have them),
   relationships and family, your path, gear, titles, body and work.
6. **World**: the map, the Times, your war (the full War Room when you have a role in it),
   politics, society, lands and organisations, the Bingo Book, justice when it applies. The hub
   screen opens filtered to the tabs that belong to World.
7. **Chronicle**: your story so far, the Chronicle, the story of this save, your line, the
   histories, Kage lines, the Times archive, case files.
8. **Contextual complexity**: one `lifeStage(c)` (child, academy, genin, chunin, jonin, anbu,
   kage, arbiter, rogue, akatsuki, retired) decides what is surfaced first. It never removes an
   action: everything stays reachable through its destination or More.
9. **Tests move into the repo** (`tests/`), so they survive container resets.

## V13.1 — The Living World (next)

- Fold the remaining duplicate entry points (the old war modal and the War Room as one War screen;
  Bingo Book, bounties, hunters and strikes as one Rogue Life screen; Tribunal and Iron Scales as
  Justice).
- Proportional notifications: minor overlays (moments, small kill flashes) go to the log; keep
  cinematic presentation for turning points.
- Explore places get inline actions instead of opening the older modals.
- Character sections become inline panels rather than links to modals.
