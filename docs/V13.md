# V13 — A Life Worth Living: interface map and migration plan

## What the play screen is today (12.4)

All of this lives in `src/ShinobiLife.jsx`, inside `ShinobiLife()`.

- **Screens**: `intro` (character creation), `play`, `dead`.
- **Play screen layers**, back to front:
  1. Background: village or era art, the chakra field, grain and vignette (`ATMOS`, `bgMode`).
  2. Cinematic overlays: investiture, Mangekyo flash, Madara scenes, Infinite Tsukuyomi,
     Sharingan awakening, "moments", kill flash, village arrival, the year odometer.
  3. One of two interface skins (`ui` preference): **The Dossier** (default) or **The Scroll**.
  4. 62 modal screens (`modal === "..."`), the battle screen (`bt`), forced decisions (`c.dilemma`).
- **The Dossier header** shows all of this at once: identity and year, a power ring, a vitals rail
  (health, standing or infamy, ryo, four action pips, Age up), a world-state ticker with five
  indicators, eight hub doors (`V11_HUBS`), about nineteen quick-jump chips, a war bar, a news bar
  and an "ongoing" strip (`situationOf`).
- **The Dossier body**: the body and the record (8 stats, about 35 conditional record lines), the
  life feed, the action deck (`acts`: "Needs you now", "This year", "The world", about 20 tiles),
  a dock (People, Profile, Age up), the Times, the world at a glance and the changelog, plus a
  floating Age up button.
- **Duplication**: the War is reachable from five places (deck tile, quick chip, war bar, hub,
  ongoing strip); the Bingo Book from four; People from three. A genin sees Kage-level tools
  (Rule, Tribunal, Court) as disabled tiles competing with the three things they can actually do.

## What already works in our favour

- Every action is data (`acts`), decided in one place.
- `situationOf(c)` already ranks everything still happening, worst first, with a `go` target.
- The hub screen (`modal === "hub"`) already groups the deep systems into Life, World, People,
  Records, Power, Politics, Society and Law.
- `goTo()` opens any screen from a target description.

## V13.0 — The New Life

A new presentation layer over the existing systems. No simulation, combat, save or story code is
rewritten; every button opens the screen or runs the function it already did.

1. **New default interface, `ui === "life"`.** The Dossier and the Scroll stay available under
   Appearance → Interface. Stored preferences are migrated once (`v13` flag), so existing players
   land in the new interface, and their choice is respected afterwards.
2. **Five destinations** replace the header clutter: Home, Explore, Character, World, Chronicle.
   Top tabs on wide screens, a bottom tab bar on phones. One compact "Advance year" control is
   always reachable. A **More** sheet lists every screen in the game, grouped by destination, so
   nothing becomes undiscoverable.
3. **Home** answers: who am I, what matters, what next. The new office art, identity and status in
   words, a life chapter (The Beginning, The Shinobi, The Path, The Legacy), **one** "Your next
   chapter" event with a primary action, up to three suggestions, up to four pending matters
   (from `situationOf`), recent developments, and Advance year with a plain list of what will
   resolve. After a year turns, a **Year in review** card shows only the most important lines.
4. **Explore**: places, not menus. The training grounds, the mission desk, the market, the
   streets, the border, the tower, the clan compound, the tribunal and court, the bounty station;
   for missing-nin the underworld and the hideout. Each place offers only its own actions.
5. **Character**: the body, abilities and advanced powers (only when you have them),
   relationships and family, your path, gear, titles, body and work.
6. **World**: the map, the Times, your war (the full War Room when you have a role in it),
   politics, society, lands and organisations, the Bingo Book, justice when it applies. The hub
   screen opens filtered to the tabs that belong to World.
7. **Chronicle**: your story so far, the Chronicle, the story of this save, your line, the
   histories, Kage lines, the Times archive, case files.
8. **Contextual complexity**: one `lifeStage(c)` (child, academy, genin, chunin, jonin, anbu,
   kage, arbiter, rogue, akatsuki, retired) decides what is surfaced first. It never removes an
   action: everything stays reachable through its destination or More.
9. **Tests move into the repo** (`tests/`), so they survive container resets.

## V13.1 — The World Answers

Three aims, from playing 13.0: use the existing systems where they live instead of opening
separate screens, make big decisions come back visibly, and test whole lives.

1. **Places work where they are.** An Explore card with something to do (training grounds,
   mission desk, streets, bounty station) opens in place across the full width and lists its
   actions as rows: regimens with their gains, named missions, D–S, the duties of the rank,
   everyone on the street with their request, open bounties. Each row calls the same function the
   old window did (`doTrain`, `startCanonMission`, `startMission`, `doDuty`, `folkUi`,
   `huntBounty`). What happened appears under the card as **Just now** (the log entries written
   since the click). The full windows remain one button away for the rarer parts.
2. **Character → Your people** lists `socialize` actions inline: teammates, sensei, rival,
   sibling, courtship, marriage, children.
3. **One War screen.** In the new interface the war modal and the War Room share a two-view
   switch (`warSwitch`). World and More show one entry for the war.
4. **One law screen.** The law hub gains the Tribunal; World shows one door.
5. **Proportional notices.** In the new interface `c.moment` and kill flashes below tier 3 render
   as a small `.v13-toast`; tier 3–4 kills keep the full-screen flash. Classic interfaces are
   unchanged.
6. **Cause and effect** on the existing Chronicle/echo engine (`chron` with `line` and `cause`,
   `echo`, `ECHOES`):
   - `milestoneTick` (yearly) notices taking the seat and leaving the village, writes a root
     entry with your name as its `line`, and schedules echoes (`seatFive`, `seatLegacy`,
     `leftBehind`, `leftLong`).
   - A field battle won big enough for the front page becomes a root and schedules
     `battlefield` (a memorial, ten years on). The festival day in your name schedules
     `festival`. Teaching the folk apprentice creates a root; their promotions are recorded with
     it as their cause, and making jonin schedules `apprenticeCell`.
   - `becauseOf(c, since)` walks each entry's `cause` chain back to a root that belongs to you
     or your line. Home shows it as **Because of you**, the year in review as **And because of
     what you did** (echo lines are told once), the Chronicle as **What you set in motion**.
     `echoesPending` counts what is still on its way.
   - Echoes and the Chronicle already pass to the relative who plays on, so a parent's
     consequences land in the child's life and are labelled as the parent's doing.
7. **Tests.** `tests/v131.cjs` covers each piece; `tests/lifefull.cjs` plays one whole life
   end to end in the new interface. The runner can keep each suite's output (`SL_LOGS=dir`),
   and page errors are recorded with the first lines of their stack.
8. **A fix the tests found.** Several systems start `c.world` as `{}` if they run before the
   first year turn; the year tick then failed on `W.wars.forEach` every year after, so the save
   could never advance. `worldEnsure(c)` now completes a half-built world wherever the game
   checks for one, which also mends saves already stuck.

## V13.2 — The Road

A missing-nin's Explore destination becomes **The Road** (`destsOf(c)` renames the tab), laid
out for desktop and stacking on narrow windows. No sidebar: the V13 top bar stays.

- **Poster** from real values: `c.bingo`, `c.bounty`, years since the leaving root
  (`c.v13m.leftRoot`), infamy in words. Three tiers: no likeness, portrait, portrait and seal.
- **The road** reuses the Explore places (`places`) for its panels: training rows in the
  hideout, bounties in the border towns, contracts (`startCrime`) in the underworld, the street
  after dark (`folkUi`). `roadClosed(c)` closes towns as infamy passes 25, 50 and 75.
- **What you left behind** reads team, sensei, family (`c.road.fam`), the folk apprentice and the
  street. `ROAD_ACTS` holds each action as `can`, `cost` and `go`; `roadDo` charges the action only
  when the move is allowed.
- **The way back**: `roadPaths(c)` gives pardon (favours of 5), debt (paid against
  `roadOwed(c)`, the bounty) and cause (battles of 3 while `roadHomeAtWar(c)`). `pardonOdds(c)`
  feeds the existing `seekPardon`: certain once a path is finished, otherwise the old odds plus
  progress. Coming home writes a Chronicle entry caused by the leaving.
- **Faces**: `probeFaces()` looks once for `img/faces/<role>[-m|-f]-<n>.webp` (roles: wanted,
  squad, sensei, family, apprentice, street); `faceSrc` picks by a hash of the name, so a person
  keeps their face. The starting set is cropped from the design mockup; place art is in
  `img/road/`.
- **Tests**: `tests/road.cjs`.
